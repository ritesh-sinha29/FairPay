# Reach & Impact Report — FairPay on Arkiv

*ETHGlobal Mumbai Hackathon Submission*

---

## 1. Who Is It For, and Would They Use It?

FairPay is built for:
1. **Web3 Freelancers & Contractors**: Developers, security auditors, and UI/UX designers who want guaranteed milestone payouts without paying exorbitant 10-20% platform commissions to centralized Web2 intermediaries like Upwork or Fiverr.
2. **DAOs & Web3 Startups**: Teams looking for streamlined Request for Proposals (RFPs) and milestone escrow contracts without building custom escrow smart contracts from scratch for every grant or work order.
3. **Autonomous AI Agents**: Multi-agent workflows that coordinate task delegation, bid evaluation, and milestone verification using Arkiv’s ephemeral state and automated expiration.

### Why They Will Use It:
- **No Stale Job Clutter**: Unlike Web2 platforms where dead job posts remain indefinitely, Arkiv’s time-to-live prunes inactive postings automatically.
- **Gas-Free Discovery**: Freelancers and AI agents can query live opportunities without spending testnet or mainnet gas.
- **Trustless Milestone Custody**: Escrow funds unlock strictly upon milestone acceptance on verified smart contracts.

---

## 2. Can They Try It Today?

**Yes!**
- The live interactive board is accessible directly at `/arkiv` in the FairPay application.
- Users can view verified testnet entities, filter by category and budget, inspect the active Arkiv SQL query, and publish their own auto-expiring milestone RFPs directly to the **Rabdi testnet** (Chain ID: `7794095`).
- Transactions and entities can be validated on the [Arkiv Rabdi Explorer](https://rabdi.explorer.arkiv.network).

## 3. Post-Mumbai Development Roadmap

We are actively developing FairPay beyond the hackathon:
- **Q2 2026**: Multi-chain deployment linking Arkiv RFP metadata with EVM escrow settlement across Arbitrum, Base, and Arc testnet.
- **Q3 2026**: AI Agent SDK integration enabling autonomous agents to discover RFPs on Arkiv, place cryptographic bids, and submit work deliverables automatically.
- **Q4 2026**: Mainnet rollout of Arkiv-indexed milestone reputation proofs for verified builders.
